UNIVERSITY LIBRARY GUIDE

1. Library Timings

The university library operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays. On Saturdays and Sundays, the library operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The library remains closed on public holidays.

2. Membership

All registered university students and faculty members are eligible for library membership. Visitors do not have borrowing privileges.

3. Book Borrowing Rules

Students can borrow a maximum of three books at a time for 14 days. Faculty members can borrow a maximum of five books at a time for 30 days.

4. Late Return Policy

A fine of 2 rupees per book per day is charged for books returned after their due date. The fine is calculated based on the number of overdue days.

5. Reference Books

Reference books are available for reading inside the library and cannot be borrowed.

6. Book Renewal

Borrowed books may be renewed once if no other member has reserved the book.

7. Library Facilities

The library provides reading rooms, computer access, Wi-Fi, and digital resources to registered members.

8. Computer Usage

Computer facilities are available to registered members. Each computer session is limited to 30 minutes when other users are waiting.
